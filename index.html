Built for real work

Where a forklift earns its keep.

A forklift is often one of the most frequently used machines in a business. Choosing around the actual workflow can matter more than simply choosing the biggest capacity.

01

Warehouses & factories

Move pallets, stack materials, load trucks and trailers, and make better use of vertical storage. Mast height, turning radius and fork adjustment are especially important here.

02

Yards & containers

Container unloading and outdoor handling place different demands on traction, tyre choice, ground conditions, clearance and operator visibility.

03

Agriculture & rural work

Outdoor loads can require greater clearance and traction. All-terrain configurations can be relevant when the work moves beyond smooth concrete floors.

04

Construction & materials

Bricks, building products, steel, machinery components and bulk materials can demand higher capacity, rugged tyres and attachments matched to the load.

Buying guide

How to choose the right forklift.

Start with the load — not the badge.

Rated capacity is one of the first numbers buyers look at, but the real operating requirement can also depend on load centre, attachment weight, lift height and the way the load is positioned.

If your heaviest load is close to the forklift's rated capacity, discuss the actual load configuration with the supplier before choosing a machine.
1

Define the loads

Record the heaviest normal load, dimensions, pallet type, load centre and whether loads are stable or irregular.

2

Measure the workplace

Check aisle width, doorway clearance, container access, turning areas, floor condition and maximum stacking height.

3

Choose the mast

Standard and premium configurations can differ in mast stages and lifting height. The right choice depends on storage and access requirements.

4

Match tyres & terrain

Indoor concrete, gravel yards and rough outdoor surfaces do not place the same demands on tyres, traction or ground clearance.

5

Plan ownership

Ask about warranty, maintenance, parts, after-sales support, delivery, finance and operator requirements before you commit.

Frequently asked questions

Forklift buying questions, answered.

What forklift capacity should I choose?

Start with your real loads, then consider load centre, lift height, attachment weight and working conditions. A supplier should confirm the configuration against your intended loads rather than relying on capacity alone.

Are forklift packages different at the same capacity?

Yes. Forklift King currently lists Standard and Premium packages for several capacities. The published differences include mast configuration, lift height, side shift, adjustable forks and lighting packages.

Does Forklift King deliver Australia-wide?

The company states that Australia-wide delivery is available. Its FAQ also says specialised carriers are used for delivery and that timing depends on location and model.

What warranty is currently advertised?

The current warranty page states a 24-month or 1,000-hour standard machine warranty, whichever comes first, subject to the published terms and exclusions. Always verify the current warranty document before purchase.

Can I view forklifts in person?

The company says its factory and display is at 103–109 Quarry Road, South Murwillumbah NSW, and recommends contacting the team to arrange a viewing.
